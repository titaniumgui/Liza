SD card reminder

EZVIZ reports the card's size but not its free space. When the card is full, the camera overwrites its oldest recordings, so Liza checks for that: if the oldest recording is newer than the day you emptied the card, it's full. Liza then knows how many days a full card holds, and reminds you before the next one fills.

Liza web
